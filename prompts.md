***PartyRock Prompts Used***
This file documents the exact prompts used to build the Migrant Wage Checker app on PartyRock, for transparency and reproducibility.
***1. Initial App Generation Prompt***
Used to generate the app skeleton (input widgets + output widget) via PartyRock's "Generate an app" feature:
"Build an app called 'Migrant Wage Checker' for Indian migrant workers to check if they are being paid fairly. The app should have these inputs: 1. State (dropdown or text: Delhi, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Punjab, Haryana, Telangana, Kerala, Odisha) 2. Job category (Construction, Agriculture, Domestic Work, Factory Work) 3. Skill level (Unskilled, Semi-Skilled, Skilled) 4. What they are actually being paid per day (in Rupees). The app should look up the correct minimum daily wage for that state, job category, and skill level from a reference data table, compare it to what the worker says they are actually being paid, and respond in simple, respectful language: If they are being paid fairly or above minimum wage, confirm this clearly. If they are being underpaid, clearly state the shortfall amount and explain they may be a victim of wage theft. Always end by mentioning they can file a complaint with the Labour Department helpline (number: 155214). Keep the tone simple and easy to understand for someone with basic literacy, not legal or technical language."
***2. Output Widget Refinement - Bilingual (Hindi + English) Response***
Added to the "Wage Fairness Result" widget prompt after initial testing, to make the app accessible to migrant workers who are more comfortable reading Hindi than English:
"IMPORTANT: Respond in simple Hindi (Devanagari script) first, followed by a short English summary. Most migrant workers understand Hindi better than English, but keep both versions available. Keep the language very simple and respectful. Use a single bilingual heading at the top that works for both language readers, like 'aapki majdoori ki jaanch / Your Pay Check'. Do NOT use separate section labels like 'Hindi Response' or 'English Summary' anywhere in the output - the Hindi and English portions should flow naturally under this one shared heading, with no internal headers separating them."
***3. Reference Data***
The app uses a built-in reference table (based on minimum_wage_data.csv in this repo) covering state x job category x skill level combinations, compiled from 2026 state labour department minimum wage notifications.
***Why This Approach***
Rather than writing custom backend logic to look up wage data and compare it against user input, the entire lookup, comparison, and tone-of-response logic is handled by describing intent in plain language to the foundation model (via PartyRock/Amazon Bedrock). This meant no traditional coding was needed to build a fully functional data-driven tool.
Built solo in 3 days for First Commit, Bharat Builds Tour.
